WES is a warehouse execution system. It receives orders from WMS, allocates inventory, creates execution tasks, and coordinates with ESS.
ESS schedules robots and equipment. WES sends transport tasks to ESS, while ESS reports task state and equipment events.
An inbound workflow normally includes receiving, container binding, putaway task creation, equipment execution, and inventory confirmation.
An outbound workflow normally includes order release, inventory allocation, picking, sorting, packing, and shipment confirmation.
In an event-sourced service, durable events reconstruct state. Commands request changes, events record accepted facts, and snapshots reduce recovery time.
Operational troubleshooting should correlate request identifiers, task identifiers, container codes, timestamps, and state transitions across WMS, WES, and ESS.
